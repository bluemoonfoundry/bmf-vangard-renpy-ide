# Vangard Studio — Feature Script: Audio Editor

Part of the per-feature deep-dive set (see `bmf-vangard-renpy-ide-whfs`).
IDs namespaced `au-*`.

**Target runtime:** ~1:10-1:30
**Tone:** practical demo-style, same as the other feature scripts.

## Script

| Time (rough) | ID | VO (narrator) | On-screen / visual cue |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:10 | au-hook | "Checking whether a sound cue actually fits a scene usually means alt-tabbing to a media player and back, over and over." | Illustrative: switching between the app window and an external media player |
| 0:10–0:22 | au-open | "The Audio Editor View lets you preview your project's music and sound effects without leaving the app." | Story Elements Audio tab / Audio Editor View opening |
| 0:22–0:38 | au-play | "Play a track right where it's listed, see the waveform, and scrub to the exact moment you're checking." | Playing a track, waveform visible, scrubbing playhead |
| 0:38–0:52 | au-organize | "Everything your project references — music, sound effects, voice lines — organized in one list instead of a raw folder of files." | Audio list showing categorized/labeled entries |
| 0:52–1:05 | au-assign | "Find the cue you want, and see (or jump to) where it's actually used in your script." | Clicking through from an audio entry to its usage in a label |
| 1:05–1:20 | au-close | "The Audio Editor — for checking your sound without breaking your flow to do it." | Audio Editor View, held |

## Notes for the VO service

- Keep this one short and light — it's a convenience feature, not a
  headline one; don't oversell.

## Capture notes

- Use a project with a handful of named, realistic audio files (not
  `track1.mp3`) so the organized list actually reads as useful on camera.
- Waveform/scrub capture may need screen recording at a higher frame rate
  than other segments to avoid choppy playhead motion.
